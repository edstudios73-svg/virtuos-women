# Virtuous Women of Destiny

Website for Virtuous Women of Destiny, a non-profit women's ministry with a missionary outreach vision. They meet every Tuesday, 9 AM – 12 Noon, at Christian Service Church, Jungle Avenue Road, East Legon, Accra.

It's a plain static site with no build step: five pages, `styles.css` and `script.js`.

## Hero photos

The home hero rotates through `assets/hero-1.webp` to `assets/hero-4.webp` (photos of Rev. Dr. Gifty Ezekiel, resized to 600px wide). `hero-3.webp` is also used on the About page.

## Deploy

Import the repo into Vercel and leave the framework preset as "Other".
