# Virtuous Women of Destiny

Website for Virtuous Women of Destiny, a non-profit women's ministry with a missionary outreach vision, founded and led by Prophetess Dr. Gifty Ezekiel. They meet every Tuesday, 9 AM – 12 Noon, at Christian Service Church, Jungle Avenue Road, East Legon, Accra.

Live at https://www.virtouswomenofdestiny.org

It's a plain static site: five pages, `styles.css`, `script.js` and `map.js` (the "Find us" map on the homepage, built with Leaflet and OpenStreetMap).

## Editing pages

The five HTML pages share a header, menu and footer, and are generated by `tools/build.py`. Edit the text there, then run:

```
python3 tools/build.py
```

## Photos

- `assets/hero-1.webp` to `assets/hero-4.webp`: the rotating homepage hero. `hero-2` and `hero-3` are also used on the About page.
- `assets/photos/`: photos from Dr. Ezekiel's Birthday Service II by BEEP Photography, used in the Gallery, the homepage and the Outreach and Contact pages.
- `assets/og-image.jpg`: the preview image shown when the site is shared on WhatsApp or social media.

## Deploy

The Vercel project is connected to this repository, so every push to `main` goes live automatically. Framework preset: "Other".
