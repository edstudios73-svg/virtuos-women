About

A sisterhood,
not a church.

Founded by Rev. Dr. Gifty Ezekiel to bring women together in faith, purpose and service.

It started as a calling. It became a family.

Virtuous Women of Destiny began with one conviction: every woman has a God-given purpose, and she shouldn't have to find it alone.

What started as a calling on Rev. Dr. Gifty Ezekiel's life has grown into a fellowship of women who support, encourage and empower one another. We are a non-profit women's ministry, not a church, and women from every church and background meet with us.

We gather every Tuesday morning at Christian Service Church in East Legon to pray, study the Word and plan how we'll serve. New to faith or walking with Christ for years, there's a seat for you.

Motto

Women of Destiny. We are highly honoured.

Mission

To help women grow strong in faith, build true friendships and serve their communities with love.

Vision

Women who live with faith, purpose, integrity and compassion, who lead well and bring change to their families and communities.

What we stand for

  1. Faith

    We stand on God's Word and follow the Holy Spirit in all we do.

  2. Purpose

    We help every woman discover and live out her calling.

  3. Integrity

    Honesty and truth, in private and in public.

  4. Compassion

    Christ's love shown through real care for others.

  5. Leadership

    Women who lead with wisdom and courage.

  6. Service

    Our time and resources, given to help others.

Common questions

When do you meet?

Every Tuesday from 9:00 AM to 12:00 Noon.

Where?

Christian Service Church, Jungle Avenue Road, East Legon, Accra, Ghana.

Do I need to belong to a particular church?

No. We're a women's ministry, not a church. Women from any church or background are welcome.

Who leads the ministry?

It was founded and is led by Rev. Dr. Gifty Ezekiel.
