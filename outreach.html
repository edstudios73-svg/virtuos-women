Outreach statement

Empowerment
through Christ.

Outreach isn't something we do on the side. It's the reason we gather.

We reach out to

  1. The unsaved

    Sharing the gospel in word and, more often, in deed.

  2. The poor

    Food, supplies and practical help for struggling families.

  3. The orphanage

    Time, gifts and a mother's attention for children waiting for a family.

  4. Prisons

    Visiting, praying, and reminding people they haven't been forgotten.

  5. Hospitals

    Sitting with the sick and praying with their families.

  6. The widows

    Standing beside women who have lost their husbands.

  7. Street children

    Meals, care and safety for children the city overlooks.

  8. Etc.

    Wherever we're needed next.

We are a family

We give

Joy

to the hopeless.

We give

Peace

to the weary.

We reach out to

The broken-hearted.

And we stay.
