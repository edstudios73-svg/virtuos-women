01 · About

Not a church. A fellowship of women who believe faith is something you do.

Founded and led by our President, Prophetess Dr. Gifty Ezekiel, we bring women together to grow in faith, find their purpose and serve the people around them. Women from any church or background are welcome.

Read our story →

Our founder

“Every woman has a God-given purpose, and she shouldn’t have to find it alone.”

Prophetess Dr. Gifty Ezekiel answered a calling to gather women in faith, purpose and service. Today she leads Virtuous Women of Destiny as its Founder and President.

Meet our founder →

Tuesday mornings

Three hours that carry us through the week.

  1. 01

    We pray

    We open the morning in worship and prayer, for each other and for the city.

  2. 02

    We study the Word

    We open the Bible together and ask what it means for the week ahead.

  3. 03

    We go and serve

    We plan who we’ll visit and how we’ll help, then go out together.
