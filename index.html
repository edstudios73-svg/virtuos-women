Who we are

Not a church. A fellowship of women who believe faith is something you do.

Founded by Prophetess Dr. Gifty Ezekiel and led by our President, Lady Pastor Kate Quashigah, we bring women together to grow in faith, find their purpose and serve the people around them. Women from any church or background are welcome.
