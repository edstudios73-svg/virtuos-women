Our story

Not a church.
A sisterhood.

Virtuous Women of Destiny was founded by Rev. Dr. Gifty Ezekiel to bring women together in faith, purpose and service. It started as a calling and grew into a fellowship of women who hold each other up.

We aren't a church. We're a women's ministry, and women from any church or background are welcome. Through prayer, fellowship, outreach and leadership training we help each woman find her God-given purpose and live it with confidence.

Our mission

To help women grow strong in faith, build true friendships and serve their communities with love.

Our vision

Women who live with faith, purpose, integrity and compassion, who lead well and bring change to their families and the world around them.
